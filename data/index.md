# Datasets

This directory contains the candidate feature data. See the [Features Catalog](../FEATURES_CATALOG.md) for what each dataset measures, its source and coverage.

| File | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `johnson_matthey_rhodium_daily.parquet` | Target: Johnson Matthey London daily rhodium, January 1996 onward; USD/troy oz and USD/lb. |
| `bds_06c4bc4f03a645bf951a92f8e59c3006__chinadata_ev_production.parquet` | Monthly China new-energy-vehicle production. |
| `bds_5529cd6bc41841d7b58fb69e90cd2546__pgm_price_history.parquet` | Daily platinum-group-metal prices. |
| `bds_59f19703af7c45bab999c0ed86851136__chinadata_vehicle_production.parquet` | Monthly China vehicle production. |
| `bds_68ec2edb578d4f93af8bc60fc0db9777__fred_zafprmito01ixobm_history.parquet` | Monthly South African total mining-production index. |
| `bds_8a05db43d9ba4fe58ec076250a56f1f9__iea_evs_eu_cars_historical.parquet` | Annual EU passenger-car EV sales, stock, and shares. |
| `bds_92934197439c46fc9622dfc440cace8f__fred_altsales_history.parquet` | Monthly US light vehicle sales. |
| `bds_fb39b19cbcc4464abc5ca4dbbdf4cf1f__un_comtrade_sa_refined_rhodium_monthly.parquet` | Monthly South African rhodium exports. |
| `bds_fed88706ea3046d4a66c7c7c7c71ef12__fred_dexsfus_history.parquet` | Daily rand-to-US-dollar exchange rate. |
| `sa_mining_production_sales_monthly.parquet` | Monthly South African mining production and sales, merged from the two source workbooks. |
| `jm_rhodium_supply_demand_annual.parquet` | Annual Johnson Matthey rhodium supply, recycling, demand, and market balance. |
| `cftc_pt_pd_open_interest.csv` | Weekly platinum and palladium futures open interest and managed-money positions. |
| `russia_geopolitical_risk_index.parquet` | Monthly Russia geopolitical-risk article-share measures. |
| `iea_china_ev_sales.parquet` | Annual China passenger-car EV sales, stock, shares, and related energy measures. |

Original attachments and filename mappings are in [sources](sources/README.md). The China IEA dataset is annual sales and related measures; the ChinaData EV series is monthly production.
